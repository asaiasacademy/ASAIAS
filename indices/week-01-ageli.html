Первый официальный выпуск индекса education и literacy. AGELI ранжирует платформы и организации по качеству AI-обучения, публичной понятности, прикладной пользе для людей и институциональной доступности материалов.

Week 01 Reading

  • DeepLearning.AI leads because its technical learning surface is deep, current and strongly aligned with real AI practice.
  • OpenAI Academy ranks second because it has become a broad public literacy hub, not only a brand microsite.
  • Khan Academy / Khanmigo stands out because it turns AI literacy into classroom-ready workflows for educators.
  • Google for Education stays close because Gemini for Education connects pedagogical use with institutional deployment logic.
  • Hugging Face Learn is unusually strong on open technical access and multilingual reach.

Methodology Snapshot

  • Index: `AGELI` — ASAIAS Global AI Education and Literacy Index.
  • Freeze date: `2026-05-12`.
  • Weights: educational clarity 25%, practical usefulness 25%, curricular depth 20%, access and reach 20%, multilingual readiness 10%.
  • Score range: `0-100`.
  • Confidence layer: `High`, `Medium`, `Low`.
