Week 01 Reading

  • OpenAI is Week 01 leader because it pairs frontier capability with the broadest current multi-tool work surface.
  • Google DeepMind stays close behind because Gemini 2.5 continues to expand in multimodal and computer-use directions.
  • Anthropic remains in the top band due to agentic coding performance and relatively visible safety framing.
  • NVIDIA ranks unusually high for a non-model lab because its stack now directly shapes agent and physical-AI infrastructure.
  • Mistral AI, Hugging Face and Meta AI form the strongest open / open-distribution cluster in Week 01.

Methodology Snapshot

  • Index: `AGESI` — ASAIAS Global AI Ecosystem Signal Index.
  • Freeze date: `2026-05-12`.
  • Weights: capability/substance 30%, cadence 20%, governance/responsibility 15%, ecosystem relevance 20%, clarity/accessibility 15%.
  • Score range: `0-100`.
  • Confidence layer: `High`, `Medium`, `Low`.
  • Official ASAIAS ranking means freeze date, methodology, evidence pass and editorial review are all stated.
