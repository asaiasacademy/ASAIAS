Сигнальные заметки

  • OpenAI Academy currently shows the strongest consumer-to-builder coverage in one public learning surface.
  • Microsoft Learn AI learning hub is strongest on role-based enterprise skilling and operational adoption pathways.
  • NVIDIA DLI leads on credentialed technical depth, workshop delivery, and applied infrastructure training.
  • Google Skills is the cleanest free beginner path with explicit generative AI fundamentals and responsible AI framing.
  • DeepLearning.AI remains one of the broadest public audiences in AI education by scale and topical coverage.
  • Hugging Face Course is strategically important because it anchors open-model literacy and practical ecosystem fluency.
