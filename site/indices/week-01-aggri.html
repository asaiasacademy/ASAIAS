Week 01 Reading

  • Anthropic leads because its frontier governance is explicit, updated and unusually legible in public.
  • Google DeepMind stays close because responsibility and frontier-safety framing remain institutionally visible.
  • OpenAI ranks in the top band due to its broad public safety and security surface, even if not all mechanisms are formalized like Anthropic’s RSP.
  • NVIDIA is unusually strong here because trust is embedded into infrastructure and physical-AI deployment language.
  • Mistral AI enters through public compliance operations rather than frontier-risk theory.
