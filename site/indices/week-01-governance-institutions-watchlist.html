Сигнальные заметки

  • OECD.AI is strongest as a comparative observatory and policy-intelligence layer.
  • European AI Office currently has the densest direct implementation relevance because it sits inside the AI Act enforcement and coordination stack.
  • UK AI Security Institute matters because it combines government access with technical evaluation and risk research capacity.
  • NIST AI RMF remains one of the clearest operational bridges from governance language to practical risk-management workflows.
  • UNESCO provides the broadest multilateral ethics legitimacy and an implementation-oriented governance observatory.
  • AI for Good is less of a regulator and more of a standards-and-capacity platform, but it has strong institutional convening signal.
