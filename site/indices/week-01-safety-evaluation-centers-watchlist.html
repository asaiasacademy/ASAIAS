Сигнальные заметки

  • OpenAI Safety Evaluations Hub currently has the strongest product-facing public eval surface.

  • Google DeepMind is strongest where safety architecture is embedded into research and governance councils.
  • UK AISI matters because it combines public evidence, frontier testing, and a government-level mandate.
  • METR is a key independent verification node for evaluating autonomous capabilities and catastrophic-risk relevance.
