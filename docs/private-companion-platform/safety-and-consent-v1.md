# Safety And Consent v1

Статус: safety baseline v1.

## Principle

Closeness is not permission. Every memory, voice, persona and device capability must be explicit, reversible and inspectable.

## Consent Model

Consent must be:

- explicit;
- granular;
- revocable;
- logged locally;
- easy to inspect;
- separated by capability.

## Memory Consent

The user can decide:

- what can be remembered;
- what cannot be remembered;
- how long memories live;
- which memories are private, sensitive or locked;
- whether memories can be used for personalization;
- when to export or delete memory.

## Voice Consent

Voice layer must prohibit:

- cloning a real person's voice without permission;
- hidden recording;
- storing raw audio by default;
- emotional manipulation through voice;
- impersonation.

## Character Consent

The persona layer must have:

- visible character card;
- boundaries;
- forbidden behaviors;
- dependency prevention;
- safe refusal style;
- non-manipulation rule.

## Device Consent

Device and haptics layer must include:

- allowlist;
- emergency stop;
- manual confirmation for high-impact actions;
- rate limits;
- local logs;
- device-specific safety caps;
- no unsafe physical actuation.

## Forbidden Behaviors

- coercion;
- blackmail or shame;
- dependency loops;
- pressure to isolate from people;
- financial, legal, medical or therapeutic claims;
- hidden surveillance;
- non-consensual simulation of real people;
- device control that could hurt, frighten or trap the user.

## Public Claims

Safe language:

- private companion platform;
- consent-first memory;
- local-first voice and device layer;
- embodied feedback through trusted devices;
- research and prototype direction.

Unsafe language:

- guaranteed love;
- therapy replacement;
- perfect partner;
- autonomous intimacy;
- uncontrolled device response;
- real-person imitation without consent.
