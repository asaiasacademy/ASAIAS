# Payment and Treasury Policy v1

Статус: draft v1.

## Principle

Money must support education, review, research and public-interest work without creating false claims, hidden incentives or privacy risk.

## Fiat First

Primary payment rails are fiat. Payment provider, invoices, tax treatment and refund process must be reviewed before regular paid operations.

## Crypto Pilot

Crypto payments are blocked by default. A crypto pilot requires:

- legal review;
- tax review;
- treasury risk review;
- refund method;
- volatility handling;
- sanctions/compliance screening;
- public disclosure that it is a pilot.

## Treasury Buckets

| Bucket | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Operations | hosting, tools, platform fees |
| Academic Work | course design, teaching, reviewer time |
| Research | bulletins, source work, lab prototypes |
| Scholarships | discounted or free access |
| Reserve | refunds, interruptions, compliance setup |

## Refund Handling

Refund rules must be visible before payment and logged in the CRM/private finance table.

## Data

Payment details are never stored in GitHub. GitHub may contain only public policy, pricing drafts and anonymized financial model notes.
