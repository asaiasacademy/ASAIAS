# What Academy Sells

Статус: finance baseline v1.

## Образование

Курсы, self-study материалы, cohorts, assignments, rubrics and learning paths.

## Просвещение

Public lectures, explainers, glossaries, open bulletins and responsible AI literacy materials.

## Участие

Community participation, workshops, cohort sessions and contributor routes.

## Практическая работа

Capstone, demos, labs and applied exercises. Практическая работа остается учебной, если отдельно не заключен service contract.

## Review

Платная проверка submitted work, evidence pack, demo and reflection.

## Сертификаты

Сертификат выдается только по результату review. Оплата покрывает проверку, а не автоматическое одобрение.

## Лаборатории

Lab participation, research sprints, open-source analysis and educational prototypes.

## Интеграция

Institutional workshops, internal academy design, knowledge operating system mapping and pilot cohort support.

## Research Briefings

Structured research updates for organizations. Briefings are educational and strategic, not legal, investment, medical or tax advice.

## Institutional Packages

Packages for teams: discovery, cohort, report, review, workshops and roadmap.

## Sponsorship / Grants

Support for scholarships, open materials, research bulletins, translations and public-interest labs.

## Что Академия не продает

- гарантированный доход;
- трудоустройство;
- аккредитацию без подтверждения;
- автоматические сертификаты;
- financial, legal, tax or medical advice;
- private student data;
- production-ready autonomous systems without separate contract and review.
