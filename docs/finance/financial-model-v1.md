# Financial Model v1

Статус: draft v1.

## Что продаем

| Offer | Unit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Self-study course | free / paid tier | первый доступ может быть бесплатным |
| Cohort participation | per student | group sessions and support |
| Work review | per submission | оплата проверки, не approval |
| Certificate review path | per candidate | includes review and decision |
| Lab participation | per sprint / membership | educational prototypes |
| Research briefing | per briefing / subscription | for teams and institutions |
| Institutional package | per organization | discovery, pilot cohort, report |
| Sponsorship / grant | per agreement | scholarships and open materials |

## Кому продаем

- individual learners;
- working professionals;
- teams;
- educational organizations;
- companies piloting AI governance;
- research and public-interest institutions;
- sponsors and grantmakers.

- research bulletin abstract;
- contribution guide;
- public source map entries.

## Fiat First

Primary payment model: fiat first through compliant payment rails.

## Crypto

Crypto is not a default payment method. It can only be considered as a reviewed pilot with documented legal, tax, treasury, refund and compliance review.

## Grants

Grants may fund open materials, scholarships, translations, research bulletins and labs.

## Sponsorship

Sponsorship must not buy academic conclusions, certificate approvals or hidden influence over review decisions.

## In-Kind Value

In-kind support may include cloud credits, software, venue, translation, expert review, design or research support. It must be logged and disclosed when relevant.

## Service Exchange

Service exchange may be accepted only with clear scope, value, owner and conflict-of-interest review.

## Куда идут деньги

- course production;
- reviewer time;
- platform and hosting;
- scholarships;
- research and source work;
- translations;
- community operations;
- legal/accounting setup when needed.

## Refund Rules

Refund rules must be visible before payment. Suggested pilot rule:

- before cohort starts: refundable minus hard payment fees;
- after cohort starts: partial refund by unused service scope;
- completed review: non-refundable because reviewer time was delivered;
- certificate not approved: no automatic refund unless service was not delivered.

## Scholarship Fund

Scholarships may cover course access, review fee or cohort participation. Scholarship decisions are separate from certificate decisions.
