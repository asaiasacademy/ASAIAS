# Ecosystem Intelligence Protocol

Статус: required operating protocol v1.

## Purpose

ASAIAS must not operate in isolation from the live AI ecosystem. External mapping, contact discipline and event intelligence are mandatory parts of the academy baseline.

## Mandatory Rule

Before public scaling, partnership outreach, cohort launch or broad media activity, ASAIAS must complete all four tasks below:

1. Build and maintain a shortlist of 100 priority AI companies, platforms, speakers, conferences and research communities with links and priority labels.
2. Review connected inbox channels and identify AI newsletters, conference updates, potential contacts and useful event signals.
3. Maintain outreach templates for companies, organizers, speakers and partners.
4. Build and refresh a priority map of contacts and events relevant to ASAIAS.

These tasks are not optional research extras. They are required operating infrastructure.

## Why This Is Required

- The academy works in AI governance, autonomous systems and AI-native education, where the external landscape changes quickly.
- Public positioning without ecosystem awareness creates reputational noise and weak partnerships.
- Outreach without templates and contact hygiene creates avoidable conflict.
- Event and speaker mapping is needed for invitations, partnerships, bulletin writing and public credibility.

## Required Outputs

Minimum required outputs:

- one tracked shortlist with 100 entities;
- one contact/event priority map;
- one inbox review pass for AI-related channels;
- one template set for non-conflict outreach.

## Communication Standard

All outreach must follow these rules:

- no spam;
- no impersonation;
- short respectful introductions;
- clear reason for contact;
- clear relevance to ASAIAS;
- opt-in follow-up only;
- written record of every outbound contact.

## Expansion Gate

The academy should not move into broad external communications, large partnership claims or multi-channel expansion until this protocol has been completed and reviewed.
